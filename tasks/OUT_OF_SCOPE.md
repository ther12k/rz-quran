# Deliberately Deferred

The following are not hidden MVP tasks: full offline lesson packs; child voice recording or automated tajwid grading; generative chatbot/RAG; native mobile builds; payments/subscriptions; social feeds/leaderboards/messaging; teacher/school tenancy; multiple guardians; additional game types and a full Qur'an reader.

Each needs a separate product decision, data/source-rights review, technical design and acceptance criteria before entering the backlog. Do not add one just because an SDK makes a prototype possible.
